WHO AM I?

My goal is to grow as a multimedia artist by continuously improving my skills in digital animation, 3D design, film, and graphic design. I want to develop my creativity and technical abilities through different projects, practices, and learning experiences.

I aim to create meaningful, creative, and engaging digital work that tells stories, communicates ideas, and connects with people. I believe that multimedia arts can be a powerful way to express emotions, share messages, and bring imagination to life.

In the future, I hope to build a strong portfolio that reflects my passion and dedication to multimedia arts. I want to explore new techniques, challenge myself creatively, and create work that I can be proud of as I continue my journey as an artist.
